A worker who says yes has not understood

Karachi's 2015 heatwave killed more than a thousand people in a single week, most of them working outdoors. The forecasts existed. They never reached the men on the scaffold.

Phoning people is the obvious fix. The harder problem is that agreeing with an authoritative voice on a phone is the ordinary human response, not the exception. A naive flow files that yes as a delivered warning, and the employer ends up holding a compliance record full of agreement that meant nothing, which is worse than holding no record at all.

So the agent never asks a question that yes can answer. It asks what you are going to do, and where you are going to sit.

What a call has to establish

Understood the warning

True only when the worker said something that shows comprehension. Their own words are stored beside the judgement, so a reviewer can check the call rather than trust it.

Stopped work, or why not

"I am paid per delivery" is the most useful thing a call can learn, and no thermometer reports it. A refusal is filed as its own outcome, never as a partial success.

Water and shade, both

A bottle in your hand with nowhere to get out of the sun is not a safe worker, so one without the other does not count as established.

Distress, which stops everything

Dizziness, nausea, confusion or having stopped sweating abandons the script on the spot and hands the call to a human. Nothing else on the list matters once that happens.

What the employer is left holding

An alert that was sent proves nothing. A call where someone said what they were going to do, in their own words, with the time and the outcome attached, is the thing an employer can produce when a regulator asks whether they acted.

Every fact starts null, meaning not established, which is a different state from false. A call that drops before the water question must never record that the worker had no water.

Languages: English, Hindi and Arabic. AssemblyAI's streaming models do not include Urdu, so Urdu speakers are served by the Hindi model. The two diverge in script and formal register rather than in "I have stopped, I have water", but it is an approximation, and it needs testing against Karachi speech before anyone relies on it.
